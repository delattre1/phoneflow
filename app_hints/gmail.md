# Gmail
- The inbox lists emails newest first; unread are bold. Tap an email to open and
  read it. The search bar is at the top — search by sender, subject or words.
- "Compose" is the "+"/pencil button at the bottom-right. Never send an email
  the owner did not ask for, and never read out verification codes or 2FA emails.
- Left menu (hamburger, top-left) switches between Inbox, Starred, Sent, etc.
