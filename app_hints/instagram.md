# Instagram
- Tabs at the bottom: Home (feed), Search/Explore (magnifier), Reels (play
  icon), Shopping/Reels, Profile (your photo, far right). "+" in the middle
  creates a post.
- To find a person: tap the magnifier (Search), tap the search field, type the
  handle, and open the account whose exact @username matches and shows a
  verified check when relevant — not a look-alike. Their grid of posts is on
  their profile; Reels and Tagged are separate tabs on the profile.
- The feed, Explore and Reels are infinite — they never end. Collect what the
  goal asks as you scroll and stop when you have it; do not wait for an end.
- Dismiss interstitials without treating them as the goal: "Turn on
  notifications", "Add to Home Screen", login/upsell sheets — close them (X or
  "Not now") and continue.
- A post caption can be truncated with "... mais"/"... more"; tap it to expand
  before reading the full caption. Record only text you can actually read.
