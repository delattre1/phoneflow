# Safari
- The address/search bar is at the BOTTOM by default (top on older versions).
  Tap it, type a URL or search terms, and press Go/Return.
- Tabs: the two-squares icon opens the tab switcher; "+" opens a new tab.
- Reading a page: scroll to read; the page text is real content, not UI.
- Dismiss cookie/consent banners and newsletter popups (Accept/close) before
  reading, and never enter the owner's credentials into a login form.
