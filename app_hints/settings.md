# Settings
- This is the iOS Settings app (grey gear icon). It is a long scrolling list of
  rows grouped into sections; each row opens a sub-screen with a ">" chevron.
- Use the Search field at the very top to jump straight to a setting by name
  instead of hunting through sections — type the setting (e.g. "Wi-Fi",
  "Bluetooth", "Battery") and tap the result.
- To go back up a level, tap the "< Back"/"< Settings" chevron at the top-left.
- Never change security, Apple ID, passwords, or payment settings unless the
  owner explicitly asked for that exact change.
