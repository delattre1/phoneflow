# TikTok
- Bottom tabs: Home (For You / Following feed), Friends/Shop, "+" (create),
  Inbox, Profile. Search is the magnifier at the top-right of Home, or a tab
  depending on version.
- Home is a full-screen ONE-video-at-a-time feed: swipe UP to go to the next
  video, DOWN for the previous. It is infinite — never wait for an end.
- To find a creator: tap Search (magnifier), type the @handle, open the Users
  tab of results, and pick the exact handle (verified check when relevant).
- A video's caption, the author @handle, and the sound are overlaid on the
  right/bottom of the video. Record the caption text you can read; do not invent
  the part hidden behind the UI.
- Dismiss login walls / "Sign up to continue" / notification prompts (X or
  "Not now") and keep going.
