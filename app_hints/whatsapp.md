# WhatsApp
- Bottom tabs: Chats, Updates (Status), Communities, Calls. Chats is the list of
  conversations, newest activity on top.
- To message someone: open Chats, tap the new-chat pencil/"+" icon, search the
  contact by name, open the chat, tap the text field, type, and send with the
  send arrow. Never send a message the owner did not ask for.
- Search inside Chats finds both contacts and past messages.
- Do not open, read aloud, or act on one-time codes, banking messages, or
  anything that looks like a login/2FA code.
