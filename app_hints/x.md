# X
- This is X, formerly Twitter. The app icon and logo are a black "X".
- To open someone's profile: tap the search / magnifier tab, type their handle
  (e.g. "elonmusk"), and tap the account whose @handle matches exactly and shows
  a verified check — that is the official one, not a look-alike.
- On a profile, the "Posts" tab lists their tweets newest first. A post with a
  pin icon is pinned and may be old — for the "latest" post, take the first
  non-pinned post at the top of the Posts list.
- A post can be text, an image or a video with a caption. Record the caption /
  text you can read; do not invent the part cut off by the screen.
