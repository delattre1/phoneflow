# X
- This is X, formerly Twitter. The app icon and logo are a black "X".
- To open someone's profile: tap the search / magnifier tab, type their handle
  (e.g. "elonmusk"), and tap the account whose @handle matches exactly and shows
  a verified check — that is the official one, not a look-alike.
- On a profile, the "Posts" tab lists their tweets newest first. A post with a
  pin icon is pinned and may be old — for the "latest" post, take the first
  non-pinned post at the top of the Posts list.
- A post can be text, an image or a video with a caption. Record the caption /
  text you can read; do not invent the part cut off by the screen.
- To follow an account: open its profile (search, exact @handle). The button
  near the top right of the profile reads "Follow"; tap it once. It then reads
  "Following" — that is the confirmation. If it already reads "Following", the
  account is already followed: do not tap it, that would open an unfollow sheet.
- To write a post: go to the Home tab and tap the round "+" / compose button at
  the bottom right; if it opens a small menu, choose "Post". Tap the text area
  ("What's happening?") and type the text exactly as the owner gave it, in one
  go, including the @mention and any link.
- While typing an @mention a suggestion list appears over the text. Ignore it
  and keep typing; do not tap a suggestion unless the owner asked for that
  exact account. A link preview card may appear under the text — that is normal.
- Publish with the "Post" button at the top right of the composer. The composer
  closing and the new post showing at the top of Home (or a "Your post was
  sent" toast) is the confirmation. Tap "Post" only once; never publish twice.
- Dismiss upsells ("Get Premium", "Turn on notifications") with X / "Not now".
