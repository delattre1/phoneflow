# YouTube
- The old "Trending" / "Em alta" tab was removed. Its current equivalent is the
  "Hype" section — labelled "Hypados" in Portuguese — a chip near the top of the
  Home feed. When a goal mentions trending/em alta/popular, go to Hype/Hypados.
- The Home/Hype feed shows roughly ONE video per screen. To collect several
  titles, `record` each title as it appears, then `scroll_to` (direction up) for
  the next — do not expect to see several titles at once.
- A "Hype points" / pontos popup or a login/upsell sheet may appear. Dismiss it
  (tap the X, or tap outside the sheet) and continue — do not treat it as the goal.
- The title shown under each video in the feed is enough to record; you rarely
  need to open the video. If you opened one by mistake, tap the down-chevron to
  minimise it or press back, and if stuck, reset_app.
